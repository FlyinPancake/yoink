Artists are the top-level organizational unit in yoink. You add artists from search results, link them to multiple providers.
yoink automatically performs a full sync of an artist's discography when you add them, pulling in all albums and tracks from linked providers.

## Linking Providers

A single local artist can be linked to identities on Tidal, Deezer, and MusicBrainz. This lets yoink combine metadata from all sources into one unified artist page.

## Monitoring

If you add a track or album whose artist is not yet in your library, yoink creates a new artist in an **unmonitored** state.
This means the artist exists in your library but their discography is not tracked or synced.

- **Unmonitored**: artist exists in your library but their discography is not tracked
- **Monitored**: yoink syncs the artist's full discography and tracks new releases

You can promote an unmonitored artist to monitored from the artist detail page.
This triggers a full sync of their discography and starts tracking for new releases.
