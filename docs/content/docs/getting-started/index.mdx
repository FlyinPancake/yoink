## Before You Start

yoink needs at least one **download source** to be useful:

- **Tidal** - accepts a self-hosted [hifi-api](https://github.com/binimum/hifi-api) proxy or uses publicly available instances
- **SoulSeek** - requires a running [slskd](https://github.com/slskd/slskd) instance

Metadata-only providers (Deezer, MusicBrainz) enrich your library with additional artist and album information but cannot download audio files. They are enabled by default and require no configuration.

## After Installation

Once yoink is running, you'll want to:

1. [Configure your providers](/docs/providers) - especially your download sources
2. [Review your settings](/docs/configuration) - audio quality, parallel downloads, lyrics
3. [Search for music](/docs/usage-guides/searching) - find and add artists to your library
